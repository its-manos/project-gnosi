# Supplementary lesson image attribution

27 sourced images and 13 original educational diagrams added on 2026-10-31. Each appears after the paragraph pair that introduces its subject, with Greek caption and credit. All use contain sizing and the existing accessible zoom viewer. No article text, quiz, answer order, lesson ID or progress rule changes.

Source imagery is public domain/CC0 or CC BY/CC BY-SA. Each derivative retains the source image licence, creator attribution and a link to the original file page; modifications are limited to resizing and JPEG encoding. The original file-page metadata and exact licence links are retained in `scripts/fixtures/supplementary-image-rights.json`. Share-alike terms apply to the image derivatives, not a claim that unrelated app code is relicensed. Attribution to institutions is not endorsement.

Historical artefacts, propaganda and modern photographs are clearly distinguished. Lascaux II is labelled a replica; the Polytechnic photograph is labelled modern; the eugenics emblem is presented as a false claim to scientific legitimacy, not a valid model. Sensitive-history images are non-graphic. A photograph supplies context, not proof of every causal claim in an article.

Original diagrams are exact illustrations of the existing lesson content. Hypothetical AI accuracy, city-density and exoplanet measurements retain their numbers and labels. No generated photographic imagery is used. Fonts are rasterized DejaVu Sans; no font binaries are redistributed. `scripts/generate-supplementary-diagrams.py` rebuilds them with Pillow and DejaVu installed.
