# World history diagrams

Twenty original ΓΝΩΣΗ educational diagrams, authored for this update with Pillow and DejaVu Sans. No video frames, third-party photographs or illustrations were copied. The diagrams summarize concepts; they are not historical artefacts, reconstructions of particular people, literal maps, or proportional timelines. Hypothetical examples are labelled in the lessons and captions.

Each inline figure links to a research source and every lesson provides its full bibliography. The credit distinguishes diagram authorship from the institution supplying factual background. Source-page URLs with `#gnosi-diagram-...` fragments are stable lookup keys in `bundledLessonImages.ts`; they are not remote image URLs. Keep both the registration and PNGs when applying this update. Assets resolve through the existing local-image and zoom path.

The font used to rasterize labels is DejaVu Sans (Bitstream Vera / DejaVu font licences); no font binary is redistributed in this update. Rebuild source: `scripts/generate-world-history-diagrams.py` and `scripts/fixtures/world-history-diagrams.json`.
