# ΓΝΩΣΗ — Lesson content refresh

The 35 expanded lessons now have web image covers with linked source/credit pages, a more engaging opening, and a tailored section that connects each topic to a concrete example or interpretation. Thirteen of the more conceptually demanding lessons also include an additional context section. Reading estimates now range from 3 to 5 minutes based on lesson depth.

Each lesson retains its existing references and now has four quiz questions, including a new reasoning question. The added questions ask readers to apply ideas, compare evidence, or make a careful inference rather than repeat a fact. Captions explain the image's relevance; source links remain available at the end of each article.

The Greek text favors direct language, brief explanations of unfamiliar ideas, and clear distinctions between established evidence and interpretation. Lessons on violence, discrimination, and historical trauma keep a respectful, evidence-based tone. The 9 previously refreshed lessons with existing image coverage are not overwritten by this patch.
