## Meaning of status
- **Not started:** only curriculum outline exists (some IDs are not yet assigned).
- **Researching:** evidence gathering in progress; not ready for readers.
- **Drafted:** content already exists in code, **not** independently re-researched under this workflow.
- **Integrated:** research-first rewrite is in code, but automated/device or editorial validation remains.
- **Validated:** automated and device checks recorded; awaiting approval.
- **Approved:** explicitly accepted by product owner after editorial review.
