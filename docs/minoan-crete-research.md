## Objective and narrative

Explain how Bronze Age Cretan palatial centres coexisted with towns, domestic production, craft workshops, administration, ritual sites and unequal regional relationships; distinguish original evidence, interpretation and modern restoration. **Five contrasting sections, 22 stable paragraphs, 4 version-1 anchored questions after paragraphs 4/10/15/20, ~1460 paragraph words and 12 minutes including pauses.** Narrative sequence: Malia court as a spatial problem → storerooms and Mu Quarter → Gournia households and Zakros harbour → ritual imagery and Knossos restoration → later Knossos as a bridge to Mycenaean studies. Opening is an analytical description, not a rhetorical mystery or a repeat of Lesson 6.
