**ID:** `aegean-bronze-age` · **Final title:** «Σπίτια, εργαστήρια και δίκτυα: το Αιγαίο της Εποχής του Χαλκού» · **Editorial status:** Integrated, not Validated or Approved. Replaces the less distinctive working title «Το Αιγαίο πριν από τις πόλεις-κράτη». **Objective:** show that agriculture, craft specialisation, exchange, differentiated communities and uneven evidence shaped the Aegean Bronze Age, without projecting later poleis backward.

## Narrative and teaching design

Material/social history rather than chronology or navigation recap: (I) agricultural and domestic production → (II) differently organised settlements at Poliochni, Lerna and Dhaskalio → (III) metal, obsidian, coastal and wider Mediterranean exchanges → (IV) the selective survival of writing and everyday work. **19 stable paragraphs, four sections, four anchored checkpoints**, after paragraphs **5, 10, 15, 18**. Approximately **1132 whitespace-separated words of paragraph prose**, plus intro and quiz; estimated **9 min** including comprehension pauses. Editorial caveat: all Early/Middle/Late period boundaries and Cycladic/Helladic/Minoan cultural labels are regional archaeological conventions, not identities.
