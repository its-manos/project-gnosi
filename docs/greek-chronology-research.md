## Thesis and editorial choices
The chapter's purpose is chronological orientation rather than an encyclopaedic history. The title replaces an abstract «χάρτης του χρόνου» subtitle with a recognisable historical promise while preserving the stable ID. Distinctive narrative: contrasts Parthenon with distant palatial chronology → overlapping Bronze Age cultures → disruptive but uneven post-palatial transformation → Early Iron Age / Geometric social change → Archaic, Classical and Hellenistic convention → synthesis of continuity, sequence and causation. 22 stable anchored paragraphs in five sections; four non-mechanical checkpoints after paragraphs 5, 11, 17 and 20. Greek prose approximately 1,200 whitespace-delimited words plus introduction; **8 minutes estimated at 165 wpm**.
