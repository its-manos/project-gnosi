## Educational aim and editorial choice
Explain how Late Bronze Age mainland palatial institutions recorded and distributed selected goods and labour, how buildings and archive contexts refine interpretations, how titles and subordinate communities suggest social hierarchies, and why a Pylos-centred reconstruction cannot be projected across Mycenaean Greece. The title foregrounds the question of *what administration counted*, not an anachronistic hunt for one king. The article uses 25 paragraphs in five different functional movements, five anchored reasoning checks after 4/10/15/20/24, and an estimated 13 minutes including questions (1366 narrative words including introduction). It opens with a material fact, not a staged mystery.
