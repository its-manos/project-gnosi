# Ancient Greece 10/30 — Το τέλος των μυκηναϊκών ανακτόρων δεν ήταν μία στιγμή
**2026-11-01 · ID:** `palace-collapse` · **Original title:** «Μετά την κατάρρευση των ανακτόρων» · **Status:** Integrated only; NOT Validated/Approved.

## Editorial choice and scope
The principal result is a distinction between palatial institutional termination and varied regional destruction, rebuilding and settlement transformation. Narrative: evidence and chronological method → Pylos → Mycenae/Tiryns → Thebes and continuities → differentiated causal hypotheses → postpalatial transition without teleology. Six sections; 27 stable paragraphs, five version-1 questions anchored after paragraphs 4, 9, 14, 21 and 27. Estimated 16 min with responses. No general tour of palace administration, no history of Ventris, no Geometric pottery/tombs/alphabets as the article's main topic.

| `pylos-burning` to `pylos-question-of-power` | Fire and preserved tablets; date proposed as late IIIB and early IIIC in different scholarship; no actual written account of destruction; settlement evidence limits | Mountjoy 1997; Jung & Kardamaki 2022; Deger-Jalkotzy 2008 |
| `mycenae-several-fires` to `argolid-lesson` | Earlier building damage, disputed late-IIIB sequence; reuse at Mycenae; postpalatial expansion and Building T at Tiryns; migration as interpretation not demonstrated origin of each household | Jung & Kardamaki 2022; Deger-Jalkotzy 2008; Mycenaean Seminar 2016–17 |
| `thebes-timing` to `trade-reorganization` | Thebes complex chronology, other centers, ceramic and economic continuity do not prove unchanged rule | Jung & Kardamaki 2022; Deger-Jalkotzy 2008 |
| `not-one-invader`, `earthquake-test` | Conflict plausible but a single attacker not demonstrated; HERACLES seismic examination at Tiryns/Midea contested the single earthquake explanation | Knapp & Manning 2016; HERACLES 2018 |
| `cave-proxy` | Mavri Trypa speleothem climate signal; a short dry event preceding Pylos but palace destruction during comparatively wetter interval; stronger aridity afterward. Proxy does not prove culprit. | Finné et al. 2017 |
| `cascade-not-proof` to `which-evidence-weighs` | Multi-factor vulnerability is interpretive model; Egyptian Sea Peoples texts cannot identify attackers at mainland Greek sites | Knapp & Manning 2016; Deger-Jalkotzy 2008 |
| `bureaucracy-ends-not-life` to `conclusion-not-empty-world` | Distinguish institutional, population and cultural continuity; Homer is not an eyewitness | Deger-Jalkotzy 2008; Knapp & Manning 2016 |
